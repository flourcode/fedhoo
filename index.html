What you’re looking at
The 100 largest contract awards matching your search — definitive contracts, purchase orders, task orders, and BPA calls awarded or funded in the last 12 months, filtered to paper that’s still active today. Data comes straight from USASpending.gov.
Why the totals look big
Dollar figures are the lifetime obligated value of multi-year contracts — not one year’s spend. And with a top-100 scope, you’re seeing the heaviest hitters in this market, not every transaction in the database.
How to search
This is a database, not Google — keep terms literal. AND intersects (DHS AND Splunk), commas mean or (cloud, cyber), and agencies resolve automatically (VA, DISA, Air Force). Or just ask Mo in plain English.
How Partner Fit scores work
Every vendor in your search is scored 0–100 as a partner, on one of two routes:
Channel & resellers (Carahsoft, CDW, SHI and peers, plus inferred license-heavy resellers) — 30 pts award velocity (active order flow), 25 agency breadth (doors already open), 25 renewal paper expiring within 18 months (your displacement windows), 20 weight in this market. No sub-flow expected — they hold the contract; you sell through them.
Primes — 30 pts sub-flow generosity (share of their dollars flowing to subs), 15 sub bench breadth, 25 expiring paper within 18 months, 15 market share, 15 contract-vehicle awards. A prime that keeps everything in-house scores low on purpose — they rarely team.
60+ strong route in  35–59 workable  <35 long shot  ·  The Traction line shows which agencies each partner already opens, by dollars.
Why it matters for entering a new market: you almost never win a new federal market cold. You enter through someone already inside — a prime that genuinely flows work to subs, or a channel holder with the vehicle and the renewal calendar. Partner Fit ranks who that someone should be, and the expiring-paper weighting tells you when to call.
One thing to know
An end date is not an RFP. Contracts renew, exercise option years, or recompete. Check SAM.gov before you build pipeline on an end date. Subaward data lags prime awards by months.
